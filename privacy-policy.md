# Privacy Policy

## Salah Silence

Salah Silence respects your privacy.

The app does not collect personal data, contacts, messages, or media files.

Permissions such as Do Not Disturb Access and Notifications are only used to automatically switch the phone to Silent or Vibrate mode during prayer times.

## Data Storage

All prayer timings and settings are stored locally on the user's device.

## Internet Usage

The app may use internet access in future updates for prayer time synchronization and app improvements.
